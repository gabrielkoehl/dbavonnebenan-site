## About me

I'm Gabriel Köhl – SQL Server DBA and PowerShell enthusiast. After years in in-house IT, where everything became increasingly predictable, I moved into consulting at the beginning of 2021. Here there are new challenges every day in different environments.

## What I do

SQL Server administration, performance tuning, migrations – the full spectrum. PowerShell for everything that can be automated. And yes, sometimes that creates new problems which then need to be solved.

## Why this blog?

The community lives from sharing. Tools like dbatools or sp_Blitz have helped me many times. Now I'm giving something back – with practical solutions instead of theoretical treatises.

Despite the quality of these big solutions, for special requirements beyond standard administration I often prefer my own approaches. sp_Blitz is excellent, no question, but for certain cases I often only need five percent of the hundred percent of metrics – but those with more depth. Similar with dbatools: the overhead is sometimes too big, so I'd rather write it myself. That way a lot more knowledge sticks, too.

## What to expect

Real stories from everyday DBA life. No perfect solutions, but ones that work. Sometimes elegant, sometimes pragmatically put together – but they get the job done.

If you've become an accidental DBA or are simply looking for pragmatic approaches: you're in the right place.

## AI and stuff

Of course I use AI tools. They make me more efficient. But it takes time until new knowledge really sticks and is production-ready. Experience still beats hype. By the way, the articles are also translated into English with AI. 🙂
